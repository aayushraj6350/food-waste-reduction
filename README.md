# Food Waste Reduction

A web-based food rescue platform designed to help reduce food waste by allowing restaurants and food shops to offer discounted food items, especially after 8 PM.

## Features

- Customer Sign Up / Sign In
- Shopkeeper Sign Up / Sign In
- Restaurant listing
- Restaurant menu browsing
- 50% food discount after 8 PM
- Add food items to order
- Checkout and payment simulation
- Order tracking simulation
- Order history
- Restaurant rating system
- Shopkeeper discount management
- Add and delete menu items
- Responsive dark-themed interface

## How It Works

### For Customers

1. Sign in or create a customer account.
2. Browse available restaurants.
3. View restaurant menus.
4. Select food items.
5. After 8 PM, eligible restaurants can offer 50% discounts.
6. Complete the checkout process.
7. Track the order.
8. Rate the restaurant after delivery.

### For Shopkeepers

1. Sign up as a shopkeeper.
2. Create a restaurant profile.
3. Enable or disable the 50% food rescue discount.
4. Add or remove menu items.
5. Customers can then view the updated menu.

## 50% Food Rescue System

Restaurants can activate a food rescue discount. When the local time reaches 8 PM, eligible menu items are automatically displayed at 50% of their original price.
